ADHD

Online therapy for ADHD

Online therapy for ADHD helps teenagers and adults understand attention, organization, impulsivity and self-criticism - and build strategies that work in real life, in English or Portuguese.

ADHD often shows up in everyday life as difficulty starting tasks, staying focused, regulating emotions, organizing priorities or dealing with frustration. It can bring a heavy layer of self-criticism, as if effort were never enough. In therapy, these experiences are met with care, without reducing a person to a diagnosis.

How therapy helps with ADHD

In online sessions, we look at how attention, routine and emotion interact, and we build strategies that fit your context - not generic advice. The aim is to reduce overwhelm, support organization and ease the guilt that often surrounds ADHD. Therapy works alongside medical and neuropsychological evaluation, never in place of it. Sessions are available in English and Portuguese.

Signs that ADHD may need support

  • Trouble starting or finishing tasks, even important ones.
  • Losing focus quickly or feeling scattered throughout the day.
  • Impulsivity in decisions, speech or spending.
  • Emotional swings and low tolerance for frustration.
  • Persistent self-criticism and a sense of underachieving.

The process respects each person's rhythm and does not replace individual evaluation. Therapy can begin from the difficulties you notice, while a formal diagnosis is made by a qualified professional.

Frequently asked questions about therapy for ADHD

Does online therapy help with ADHD?

Yes. Online therapy helps teenagers and adults with ADHD build practical strategies for focus, organization, time and emotional regulation, by video call and with full confidentiality. Do I need a diagnosis before starting therapy for ADHD?

No, you do not need a formal diagnosis to begin.
