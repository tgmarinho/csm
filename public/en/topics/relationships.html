Relationships

Online therapy for relationships and family

Online therapy for relationships is a space to reflect on boundaries, communication, attachment, family patterns and emotional responsibility - in English or Portuguese.

Relationships can be a source of support and also of pain. The same bonds that hold us can bring conflict, distance, resentment or a sense of repeating the same story with different people. Family ties, in particular, often carry old expectations and roles that are hard to name on your own.

How therapy helps with relationships

In online sessions, we look at the patterns that repeat, the way you communicate and set limits, and the attachment styles shaped by your history. The aim is not to assign blame, but to help you relate with more clarity, care and emotional responsibility. Individual therapy can do this valuable work even when the other person is not present. Sessions are available in English and Portuguese.

What we can look at together

  • Difficulty setting or respecting boundaries.
  • Recurring conflict or communication that breaks down.
  • Patterns of distance, dependence or jealousy.
  • Family roles, expectations and old wounds.
  • Grief, separations and endings of important bonds.

The process respects each person's rhythm and does not replace individual evaluation.
