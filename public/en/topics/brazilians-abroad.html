Brazilians abroad

Online therapy for Brazilians abroad

Online therapy offers Brazilians living abroad a confidential space in Portuguese or English to talk about homesickness, adaptation, identity and emotional life away from Brazil.

Living outside Brazil can be meaningful and also emotionally complex. Distance from family, language barriers, cultural adaptation, professional transitions and the pressure to make everything work may weigh heavily, even when the move was chosen and desired.

Why language matters in therapy

Being heard with attention to your cultural background can help access memories, emotions and nuances that are difficult to explain when life is divided between places, languages and references. Here, the focus is the emotional experience of migration and the sense of belonging.

What we can work on together

  • Homesickness and feeling between cultures.
  • Loneliness and distance from your support network.
  • Stress related to adaptation, work or language.
  • Guilt about being away from family.
  • Identity, belonging and the pressure to succeed abroad.

Sessions are online, from any country, with appointment times agreed according to your time zone whenever possible. The process respects each person's rhythm and does not replace individual evaluation.

Frequently asked questions about therapy for Brazilians abroad

How does online therapy work for Brazilians abroad?

Therapy takes place by video call from any country, with confidentiality and clear agreements. Care considers homesickness, cultural adaptation, loneliness, belonging and identity.

Can sessions consider my time zone?

Yes. Appointment times are agreed considering the country and time zone where you live, so the session can fit your routine abroad whenever possible.
