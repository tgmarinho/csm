Depression

Online therapy for depression

Online therapy for depression offers ethical, gentle care for low energy, isolation, emotional pain and persistent discouragement - in English or Portuguese.

Depression is not weakness or a lack of effort. It can appear as deep tiredness, loss of interest in things you once enjoyed, a heavy sense of emptiness, trouble sleeping or a quiet withdrawal from the people around you. Naming this pain out loud, with someone who listens carefully, is already a step.

How therapy helps with depression

In online sessions, we create a private space to understand what you are going through, give shape to the suffering and organize small, possible next steps. The work respects your pace and does not rush you toward feeling better on a schedule. Therapy can also work alongside medical care when needed. Sessions are available in English and Portuguese.

Signs that depression needs care

  • Persistent sadness, emptiness or discouragement for weeks.
  • Loss of interest or pleasure in usual activities.
  • Low energy, changes in sleep or appetite.
  • Withdrawing from people and daily commitments.
  • Harsh self-criticism or a sense of hopelessness.

The process respects each person's rhythm and does not replace individual evaluation. In case of risk or emergency, contact your local emergency services immediately.

When should I seek help for depression?

Seek help when low energy, loss of interest, sadness or discouragement persist for weeks and affect sleep, work or relationships.
