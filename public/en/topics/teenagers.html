Teenagers

Online therapy for teenagers

Therapy offers teenagers a space to be heard and supported while dealing with emotions, self-esteem, conflicts, school pressure and identity - in English or Portuguese.

Adolescence brings fast changes in the body, emotions, friendships, family roles and school demands. Anxiety, low self-esteem, social difficulties or family conflict may appear as irritability, isolation, falling school performance or a feeling that everything is too much.

How therapy helps teenagers

In online sessions, the teenager has a careful space to understand emotions, build language for what feels confusing and develop healthier ways to face pressure. The process considers development, family context, ethical boundaries and the teenager's own rhythm.

Signs that support may be useful

  • Frequent anxiety, sadness or irritability.
  • Social isolation or withdrawal from family.
  • Drop in school motivation or performance.
  • Low self-esteem and intense self-criticism.
  • Recurring conflict at home or with friends.

For minors, therapy starts with responsible consent and clear agreements with the family. The process does not replace individual clinical evaluation.

Frequently asked questions about therapy for teenagers

Does online therapy work for teenagers?

Online therapy can be a good format for teenagers, especially when there is privacy, responsible agreements and family participation when needed. The work supports emotional organization, anxiety, self-esteem and conflicts by video call.

Do parents or guardians participate?

For minors, a parent or guardian participates in the initial agreement and gives consent. The content of sessions is handled with confidentiality and care, respecting ethical limits and the teenager's trust.
