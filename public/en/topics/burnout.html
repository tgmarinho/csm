Burnout

Online therapy for burnout and emotional overload

Online therapy for burnout supports people carrying the weight of routine, work, exhaustion and constant self-demand - helping you recover energy at a realistic pace, in English or Portuguese.

Burnout builds slowly. It can start as tiredness that no weekend seems to fix, then grow into irritability, difficulty concentrating, a sense of emptiness at work and the feeling of running on autopilot. Often it is fed by chronic overload and a demand to always do more, until the body and mind ask for a stop.

How therapy helps with burnout

In online sessions, we look at your boundaries, workload, emotional signals and the patterns that keep the overload going. Together we seek realistic changes that respect your life context, rather than adding one more impossible goal. The aim is to protect your energy, restore meaning and rebuild a sustainable relationship with work and rest. Sessions are available in English and Portuguese.

Signs that burnout needs care

  • Exhaustion that rest and time off no longer restore.
  • Cynicism, detachment or loss of meaning at work.
  • Difficulty concentrating and a drop in performance.
  • Irritability, sleep problems or physical tension.
  • Guilt whenever you try to slow down or set limits.

The process respects each person's rhythm and does not replace individual evaluation. In case of risk or emergency, contact your local emergency services immediately.

Does online therapy help with burnout?

How do I know if it is burnout or just tiredness?

Ordinary tiredness eases with rest, while burnout is a persistent exhaustion tied to chronic stress, often with cynicism, detachment and a drop in performance that rest alone does not fix. Therapy helps you tell the difference and act early. In case of risk or emergency, contact your local emergency services immediately.
