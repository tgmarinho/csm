About the professional

Nice to meet you. I am Carla Suzana Marinho.

Clinical psychologist, neuropsychologist and cognitive behavioral therapist.

I hold a Psychology degree from UFGD, a Neuropsychology specialization from IBNeuro and training in Cognitive Behavioral Therapy from PUC-PR. For more than 12 years, I have supported teenagers, adults and families in mental health care.

My background also includes Human Development and Learning training at Unesp - Bauru and many years of work with children, teenagers, families and school contexts. This experience broadened my understanding of human development, family relationships, behavior, learning and emotional health across different stages of life.

Over the years, I have accompanied people at different moments of their stories, both in Brazil and abroad, working with Brazilians and foreigners in different cultural contexts. This experience broadened my perspective on adaptation, belonging, identity and the construction of new paths.

Beyond psychology, I am a wife, mother, Christian and someone who deeply believes in people's potential for growth, maturity and transformation.

Before scheduling

Frequently asked questions

How does online therapy work?

Sessions are held by video call, with the same care, warmth and confidentiality as in-person therapy. You only need a private setting and a good internet connection (and headphones if you prefer).

How do I schedule a session?

Just get in touch through WhatsApp. I will be glad to talk with you, answer your questions and check available appointment times.

Do you work with teenagers?

Yes. Care is adapted to the teenager's stage of development and carried out in partnership with the family whenever necessary, while preserving confidentiality and the therapeutic bond.

Do you work with Brazilians living outside Brazil?

Yes. I work with Brazilians living in different countries, offering online therapy in Portuguese and respecting the emotional and cultural challenges that are part of this experience.

Can sessions be held in English?

Yes. I also offer sessions in English when that is the best form of communication for the patient.

How long are sessions?

Sessions last about 50 minutes and usually take place once a week. Frequency is defined according to each case's needs.

Do you accept health insurance?

Currently, sessions are private. If your health insurance offers reimbursement for psychological consultations, I can provide the required documentation for your request to the plan.

Do I need to be in Dourados to do therapy?

No. Therapy is online and available for people anywhere in Brazil and abroad. Being in Dourados/MS is only needed for the ADHD assessment, which is in person.

How does therapy for Brazilians abroad work?

Sessions take place by video call, in Portuguese, at a time arranged for your time zone. Care considers the challenges of adaptation, belonging and distance that come with living abroad.

What is the difference between online therapy and in-person ADHD assessment?

Online therapy is an ongoing process of support by video call. The ADHD assessment is a specific process, with its own steps and instruments, carried out in person in Dourados/MS.

Can I do therapy in English?

Yes. Sessions can be held in English when that is the best form of communication, and the language can be agreed on or alternated throughout the process.
